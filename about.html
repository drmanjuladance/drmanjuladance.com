About the school

A thoughtful space for classical dance.

Rooted in tradition and guided by disciplined practice, Manjula’s School of Indian Classical Dance supports learners as they build a meaningful connection with the art.

Our Philosophy

At Manjula’s School of Indian Classical Dance, we follow the Kalakshetra bani, placing great importance on mastering the fundamentals of Bharatanatyam.

We believe that strong basics are the foundation of great dancing. Through disciplined practice and dedicated guidance, students develop precision, balance, grace and confidence.

When your basics are strong, your possibilities are limitless. A solid foundation allows every dancer to explore movements, express emotions and grow in their artistic journey.

Our philosophy is simple: Master the basics, and let your dance flourish.

Learning at Our School

Our current structured group programmes focus on Bharatanatyam for adult learners, with a 12-week Starter Programme and a 12-week Intensive Programme. Both are delivered live online in English and designed for learners with no previous dance experience.

Our approach prioritises clear instruction, guided repetition, practice between classes and gradual development of technique and expression. Individual classes may also be discussed by enquiry.

Special Choreography & Performance Preparation

Preparing for a stage performance, cultural celebration or special event? We welcome enquiries from students and dance enthusiasts seeking choreography and preparation support.

Dance concept, performance duration, timeline and coaching arrangements can be discussed according to your goals, experience and availability.
