Bharatanatyam Foundation Programmes.

Begin your learning journey through structured online group classes, dedicated guidance and progressive practice.

12 WEEKS · 12 LIVE SESSIONS

Foundation — Starter Programme

Designed for adult learners aged 18 and above who are new to Bharatanatyam. No previous dance experience is required.

  • One 60-minute class per week
  • Live online classes via Zoom
  • Classes conducted in English
  • Foundational posture, Namaskaram, selected Adavus, rhythm, Mudras and introductory Abhinaya
  • Practice guidance and learning materials

The programme provides an introduction to the art form and does not promise professional proficiency in 12 sessions.

12 WEEKS · 24 LIVE SESSIONS

Foundation — Intensive Programme

A more intensive option for adult beginners who would like more frequent classes and opportunities for guided practice.

  • Usually two 60-minute classes per week
  • Live online classes via Zoom
  • Classes conducted in English
  • Fundamentals, rhythm, selected Adavus, Mudras and introductory Abhinaya
  • More repetition, correction and consolidation

The programme supports foundational learning and does not guarantee professional proficiency in 24 sessions.

Ask About Individual Classes ↗

What to expect

Classes are delivered live online via Zoom. Learning materials may include PDF notes, practice instructions, Mudra reference sheets, approved practice recordings and a personal progress sheet. Recordings of attended classes are available for 30 days.

Class schedules are planned based on enrolment interest and preferred time zones. Exact batch timings and fees are shared during enquiry.

Students are informed before joining that classes are recorded. Recordings are for enrolled students’ learning and may be downloaded during the 30-day access period. They must not be redistributed or published without permission.

Completion certificates, where offered, are subject to the programme’s attendance and completion requirements, which will be communicated before enrolment.

Other dance forms and workshops

Enquiries about Mohiniyattam, Kuchipudi, Semi-Classical Dance and workshops may be considered according to interest and availability. These are not currently presented as regular scheduled course offerings.
