Meet your guru

Experience, artistry and a deep respect for tradition.

Dr. Devi Manjula R is an Ayurvedic physician and Indian classical dancer with 29 years of dance experience. She holds an MFA in Bharatanatyam and has trained in classical dance under respected gurus.

Her artistic journey includes more than 550 stage performances, teaching experience, and over a decade of experience as an arts festival judge. Through her own dance school and online classes, she shares her passion for Indian classical dance with learners near and far.
