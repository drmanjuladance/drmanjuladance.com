Meet Your Guru

Guidance shaped by years of dedication.

Dr. Devi Manjula is an Ayurvedic physician and Indian classical dancer with 29 years of dance experience. Her training includes study at Regatta School in Thiruvananthapuram, an MFA in Bharatanatyam from Annamalai University, and advanced training under Dr. Smt. Saroj Vaidyanathan.

Her journey includes performances on many stages, teaching experience, and judging at school and college arts festivals. Online classes bring this experience to students across the world.
