Your information matters

Privacy Policy.

How we handle enquiries and online class recordings.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Enquiry Information

When you contact us, we may receive your name, email address, WhatsApp number, country, time zone, dance experience, programme preferences and other details you voluntarily share. Enquiries submitted through the website open a prepared WhatsApp message; you review and send it yourself. The school may save enquiry details for responding and coordinating potential enrolment. We do not share enquiry information with other parties, except where disclosure is required by applicable law.

2. Online Class Recordings

Live online classes are recorded for learning and revision. Students are informed before joining that recording will take place. Recordings are stored on Google Drive and made available to students participating in the relevant class for 30 days. Students who wish to keep a copy may download it during this period. Recordings are removed from Google Drive after 30 days. This does not automatically delete any copy downloaded by a student or guarantee deletion from every possible backup.

Recordings are for enrolled students’ personal learning. They must not be redistributed, shared with unauthorised persons or published on social media without permission.

3. Use of Information

Information is used to respond to enquiries, communicate programme details, coordinate schedules, manage enrolment and provide learning-related support. We do not sell personal information.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use Zoom, Google Drive, WhatsApp, email and Instagram to conduct classes, store recordings and communicate. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information entrusted to us. Enquiry details are retained as needed for their intended purpose and applicable requirements. Recordings are removed from Google Drive after 30 days.

6. Photographs and Videos

Student photographs, videos or other identifiable student content will be published by the school only with appropriate permission.

7. Cookies

This static website has been prepared without adding analytics or advertising tracking scripts. Hosting platforms or linked third-party services may use their own technical cookies or similar technologies. This statement should be reviewed if analytics, embedded services or tracking tools are added later.

8. Privacy Requests

You may contact us to request correction or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable legal and administrative requirements.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when services or information-handling practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
